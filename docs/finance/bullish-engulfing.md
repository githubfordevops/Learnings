# 📈 Bullish Engulfing at Daily Support  
*A Practical Price Action Guide*

---

## 1️⃣ What Is a Bullish Engulfing Candle

A **bullish engulfing** candle occurs when:
- A green (bullish) candle **fully engulfs** the body of the previous red (bearish) candle
- Buyers overpower sellers completely

**What it means:**  
> Control shifts from sellers to buyers.

📌 The candle body matters more than the wick.

---

## 2️⃣ What “Daily Support” Really Means

Daily support is **not a single line**, but a **zone** where price has reacted before.

### Strong daily support characteristics:
- Multiple historical touches
- Strong rejections (long lower wicks / impulsive green candles)
- Psychological levels (round numbers like 100, 500, 1000)

📌 Zones > exact prices.

---

## 3️⃣ Ideal Conditions for This Setup ✅

This setup works best when **most of these align**:

- 📍 Bullish engulfing forms **at a daily support zone**
- 📉 Prior trend was **down or corrective**
- 🕯️ Engulfing candle closes **near its high**
- 📊 Volume is **higher than previous candles**
- 🚫 No immediate resistance overhead

If these conditions align → **high probability trade**

---

## 4️⃣ Entry Strategies

### 🔹 Conservative Entry (Safer)
- Enter **above the high** of the engulfing candle
- Confirms continuation

### 🔹 Aggressive Entry (Better R:R)
- Enter near **50% retracement** of the engulfing candle
- Higher reward, higher risk

📌 Beginners should start with **conservative entry**.

---

## 5️⃣ Stop-Loss Placement (Mandatory)

Choose one:

- Below the **low of the engulfing candle**
- Below the **entire support zone** (preferred)

📌 If price breaks support again → trade idea is invalid.

---

## 6️⃣ Target Planning 🎯

Always plan targets **before entering**.

### Target options:
- 🎯 Target 1: Nearest daily resistance / recent swing high
- 🎯 Target 2: 2R–3R (risk-to-reward)
- 🎯 Trail for trend continuation if structure supports it

📌 Never enter if upside space is limited.

---

## 7️⃣ Multi-Timeframe Confirmation (Pro Technique)

- **Daily:** Identify support + engulfing
- **15-min / 5-min:** Higher low or consolidation
- **1-min:** Breakout entry (optional)

📌 This filters many false engulfing signals.

---

## 8️⃣ When NOT to Trade This ❌

Avoid this setup when:

- Engulfing forms in **sideways/choppy market**
- Very **low volume**
- Strong resistance just above
- After a **sharp, vertical fall** (needs base first)
- During major news/events

---

## 9️⃣ Common Mistakes Traders Make

- Buying every engulfing candle blindly
- Ignoring higher timeframe resistance
- Placing stop-loss inside the support zone
- Expecting reversal immediately without confirmation

---

## 🧠 Pro Insight

> A bullish engulfing at daily support is **not a buy signal**  
> It is **permission to look for long trades**

---

- [ ] Strong engulfing close
- [ ] Acceptable volume
- [ ] Clear upside space
- [ ] Minimum 2R reward
